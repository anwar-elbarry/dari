# RiadTax — logo concepts

Five directions for the "R" mark, all vector (SVG, text converted to outlines, no font needed). Same palette as `brand/README.md`: orange gradient `#ff8a1e` → `#ff5a00`, night `#0f0f10`, white "Riad" / orange "Tax", tagline "Legal & Tax Compliance".

Open `overview.svg` in a browser to compare them side by side.

| # | Concept | Idea | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Badge R | Bold R with a check-and-bars badge on the foot of the stem. Closest to the reference. | `concept-1-badge-r*.svg` |
| 2 | Check-leg R | The leg of the R is a check mark: "R, validated". Cleanest, best at small sizes. | `concept-2-check-leg-r*.svg` |
| 3 | Door R | The bowl is a horseshoe arch (riad door) holding the check; rising bars in the stem. | `concept-3-door-r*.svg` |
| 4 | Shield R | R cut out of a shield, sealed by a check: protection and compliance. | `concept-4-shield-r*.svg` |
| 5 | Khatam R | R at the centre of the eight-point Moroccan star (zellige), like a seal. | `concept-5-khatam-r*.svg` |

Per concept: `concept-N-….svg` (dark background), `…-light.svg` (white background), `…-icon.svg` (app icon, 64 × 64).

Regenerate: `pip install fonttools && python3 generate.py`.
